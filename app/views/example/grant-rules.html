Woodland creation grant rules

This is example guidance for a made-up grant.

Who can apply

You can apply if you own the land or manage it as a tenant.

  • the area must be at least 1 hectare
  • you must keep the trees for at least 15 years

What the grant pays for

The grant pays for trees, tree protection and fencing.

How much you can get

Up to £8,500 per hectare for planting, and £350 per hectare each year for maintenance.
