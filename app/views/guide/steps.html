Steps

Each screen in a journey is a step. A step is one line in the journey’s steps list.

What each part of a step does
Field What it does Needed?
type Which wireframe to show – see screen types Yes
title The label above the screen Yes
live The page’s address on the live service, shown as plain text No
prototype The page in this prototype – shown as a link, and the title links to it too. See linking to prototype pages. No
note A short note shown under the screen No

If you do not need a field, leave it out or leave it empty, like "prototype": "". It will not be shown.

Adding a step

  1. Find the journey in the journeys list.
  2. Copy the line of a step near where the new one goes.
  3. Paste it in the right place – steps are shown in the order they are in the list.
  4. Change the type, title and any other fields.

Step numbers are added for you, so you do not need to renumber anything.

Removing or moving a step

Delete the line, or cut it and paste it somewhere else in the list.

! Warning Every step needs a comma at the end except the last one. If you remove or move the last step, check the commas.

Writing notes

Keep notes short – one sentence is best. Use them for things the wireframe cannot show, such as:

  • what the user does on the page, like “User chooses how to add the planting area”
  • that the screen is a modal, not a separate page
  • a link to an optional page, like “User can optionally read the grant rules, see pages outside the main flow”
